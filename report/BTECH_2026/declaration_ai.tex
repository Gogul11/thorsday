\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{\bfseries DECLARATION ON THE USE OF AI TOOLS}

Generative artificial intelligence tools were used in this project in a limited and assistive capacity. Their use was restricted to the following purposes:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Literature-survey support:} ResearchRabbit and Elicit, together with AI-assisted language tools, were used to discover, organise, and suggest research papers and other scholarly sources relevant to the themes discussed in the literature survey. The relevance, accuracy, bibliographic details, and applicability of all sources were independently assessed and verified by the authors before inclusion. The Elicit literature-review artifacts used during this process are identified below:
\begin{itemize}
\raggedright
\item Context paging for agent memory: \url{https://elicit.com/artifact/c6d5e861-97d3-4c92-a45d-26869f67acd0?share_ref=05ce4385-30d3-425c-9a67-419bfe724f39}.
\item Multi-agent priority-aging scheduling: \url{https://elicit.com/artifact/9dda3c60-9276-4f35-891a-5ff2a38df6d9?share_ref=f4bf66b1-b743-4bcc-919e-b916fd7fae87}.
\item LLM multi-agent planning and workflow optimization: \url{https://elicit.com/artifact/d99853bf-b74b-4ec6-9564-2fb15eb8e9cf?share_ref=4cb79663-65f3-40b9-88f2-52fe0f3bdc34}.
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Language and editorial support:} AI-assisted tools were used to review grammar, spelling, sentence structure, clarity, and academic style in the report. The tools supported editorial refinement only; the technical content, interpretation of results, methodological decisions, and conclusions were developed, checked, and approved by the authors.
\item \textbf{Synthetic test-case support:} AI-assisted tools were used to propose relevant synthetic test cases for evaluating context paging, planner behaviour, and related runtime scenarios. The authors defined the evaluation objectives and acceptance criteria, reviewed the generated candidates, removed unsuitable or duplicated cases, and retained only cases consistent with the approved experimental protocol.
\end{enumerate}

The AI tools were not used to fabricate experimental results, generate unverified references, replace the authors' analysis, or determine the conclusions of the project. The authors retain full responsibility for the originality, accuracy, integrity, and final presentation of the report.

Tools used: OpenAI ChatGPT-4 GO, ResearchRabbit, and Elicit, used under the supervision and review of the authors.
